# Policy templates for legal review (not legal advice)

## Marketplace terms
Tholuwa Hat provides discovery and communication tools for independent sellers and customers. Unless separately implemented and stated in writing, the platform does not operate a delivery fleet, guarantee seller identity, or certify product safety. Sellers are responsible for lawful listings, accurate descriptions, stock, pricing, fulfilment, and required licences. Customers should review seller terms and item details before ordering.

## Privacy notice
Explain data collected (phone, profile, chosen district/locality, order records, optional device location, media and support messages), purposes, legal basis, providers, retention, security, grievance contact, and deletion/export process. Request location only when needed for nearby search. Never publicly reveal a customer's precise live location. Replace this template with a reviewed notice and actual provider disclosures before launch.

## Subscription and cancellation
Show the first subscription amount of ₹120 and monthly renewal amount of ₹99 before payment. State the subscription period, renewal behavior, taxes if applicable, cancellation process, expiry effects, and refund eligibility. No auto-debit is implied unless a valid mandate has been approved and enabled. Activate subscriptions only after server-side verified payment.

## Seller rules
Sellers must provide truthful shop and product information, honour accepted orders, maintain inventory and comply with applicable laws/licences. Prohibited, counterfeit, unsafe, or unlawful goods may be removed. Repeated abuse may result in suspension after a fair review process.

## Community standards and complaints
No illegal goods, threats, harassment, impersonation, spam, or copyright infringement. Provide report/block, moderation appeal, copyright complaint, and grievance escalation channels. Publish a monitored contact address and response timelines before launch.

## Referral eligibility
A ₹20 reward is conditional on an eligible new seller completing their first verified ₹120 shop payment and passing anti-fraud checks. Self-referrals, duplicate/fake accounts, reversed payments and manipulated transactions are ineligible. Withdrawal requests require an available balance of at least ₹500 and are subject to review and payout processing; do not promise instant payment. Define a clear expiry period and grievance route before launching.
